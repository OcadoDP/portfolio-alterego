A NOTE ON THIS VERSION / 01

Two designs. One body of work.

I wanted a darker portfolio—not simply a dark mode.

The clean portfolio remains my primary, professional presentation: clear, direct, and built for easy browsing. This Alter Ego is a separate creative interpretation of the same work, with a darker visual language, a different layout, cinematic effects, and more interactive details.

I could have added a theme toggle to the original. But the point was to explore how the same projects and experience could feel in a completely different interface—not to replace the original or change the substance of the work.
